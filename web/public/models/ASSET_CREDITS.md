# Vehicle assets

The seven vehicle GLB models and their palette texture in `cars/` are from
**Car Kit v3.1 by Kenney**: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0. The original license is included in
`cars/License.txt`. Downloaded 2026-10-03 from the author's website.

The renderer normalizes models to the driving environment's actual vehicle
dimensions and recolors body paint at runtime. Scenery, road, lighting, vehicle
feedback and interface are generated by this project. No asset requires a CDN
or a third-party request during gameplay.
